Multi-provider orchestration, rendered live

Every other AG-UI demo assumes one client and one provider. CAO is neither — it orchestrates many providers at once. This dojo shows a real kiro_cli supervisor delegating to a claude_code developer and a codex reviewer, rendered through the AG-UI stream.
